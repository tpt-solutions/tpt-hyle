# Code of Conduct

tpt-hyle is an open project. We want it to be a place where people can
contribute, disagree, and learn without fear of harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be clear about what you mean, and ask when you are unsure.
- Accept that a design decision may not go your way. Say why you disagree,
  then move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any channel.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider unprofessional or
  hostile in a shared technical space.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub, using a private
security advisory on the repository. Maintainers will review every report,
respond with care, and may remove content, warn, or ban a participant
depending on the severity and history.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when acting for the
project in public.
